# Governance

## Mission
A public archive of cybersecurity research from the Arab world and Africa:
talks, tools, CVEs, papers, and the events where they happened. An archive,
not a ranking. Celebration, not competition.

## Inclusion criteria (works)
A work is eligible if ALL of the following hold:
1. It is public (published talk, released tool, assigned CVE, published paper).
2. At least one author is from, based in, or professionally rooted in the
   covered region (self-identification is accepted and never interrogated).
3. It is security research or security tooling in a broad technical sense.

No judgment of quality, employer, nationality, or politics. Editorial
selection is limited to relevance and public availability.

## People: consent model
- **Work entries** are indexed by default — they are public record.
- **Person entries** start as unclaimed stubs: display name, optional country,
  linked works. Nothing else.
- **Claimed profiles** (verified by the person) may add bio, photo, links,
  Arabic-script name.
- **Country field is always optional** and removed on request without question.

## Claim verification
A claim is accepted when the claimant proves control of an identity already
publicly tied to the work, via ONE of:
- A post from a linked social/professional account referencing the claim PR.
- A DNS TXT record or a file on a personal site referenced in the claim.
- Direct confirmation from an email address published in the original work.
Maintainers record the method in `claim_verification` (never rendered).

## Removal & corrections
Any person may request, with no justification required:
- Removal of their person entry (works remain, attributed to name-only text).
- Full removal of name from specific entries (replaced with "researcher
  requested anonymity").
- Corrections of any field.
Requests are honored within 7 days. Contact: <maintainers email> or a GitHub
issue with the `takedown` label (issue may be filed anonymously).

## Safety
We recognize that in parts of the covered region, public association with
offensive security research can carry legal or personal risk. Therefore:
- We never add personal data (photo, bio, socials, employer) without a claim.
- We never publish location beyond an optional country code.
- We honor pseudonyms/handles as valid display names.
- Takedowns are processed without debate, ever.

## Maintainers
Decisions are made by the maintainer group listed in README. The project
aims for a majority of maintainers based in the covered region.
